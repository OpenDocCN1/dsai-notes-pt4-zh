
# 年初五迎接财神爷

> 来源：[https://xmgtoagi.feishu.cn/docx/OYZPdp09joYIlrxJfWIcgVNvn1f](https://xmgtoagi.feishu.cn/docx/OYZPdp09joYIlrxJfWIcgVNvn1f)

```
&quot;Recreate the imaginative scene of the traditional Chinese God of Wealth holding a gold ingot and riding a motorcycle at high speed, all from the distorted, wide-angle view of a door&#x27;s peephole, in a classical Chinese painting style. This unique perspective should capture the God of Wealth in traditional attire, with his robes fluttering as he rides, and the motorcycle stylized to match the aesthetic of traditional Chinese ink and wash painting. The peephole&#x27;s fisheye effect should add an interesting distortion to the scene, with a background that suggests movement and speed through abstracted classical Chinese landscape elements.&quot;
```
