## Intel CPU微架构

+ Nehalem(2008)
+ Sandy Bridge(2011)
+ Ivy Bridge(April 28, 2012): 22 nm shrink of the Sandy Bridge microarchitecture
+ Haswell(June 3, 2013): 22 [nm](http://ark.intel.com/products/81706/Intel-Xeon-Processor-E5-2660-v3-25M-Cache-2_60-GHz) microarchitecture [ficuscolombia，Intel(R) Xeon(R) CPU E5-2660 v3 @ 2.60GHz]
+ Broadwell(September 2014): 14 nm [shrink of the Haswell microarchitecture](http://ark.intel.com/products/92981/Intel-Xeon-Processor-E5-2630-v4-25M-Cache-2_20-GHz) [NIST测试服务器，IntelR Xeon(R) CPU E5-2630 v4 @ 2.20GHz]
+ Skylake(August 5, 2015): new [14 nm](http://ark.intel.com/products/88196/Intel-Core-i7-6700-Processor-8M-Cache-up-to_4_00-GHz) microarchitecture [我的工作站的CPU，Intel(R) Core(TM) i7-6700 CPU @ 3.40GHz]

具体架构介绍可以见 [wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Intel_CPU_microarchitectures)。
