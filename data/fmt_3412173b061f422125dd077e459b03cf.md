## Chiaroscuro: Strong contrasts between light and shadow for drama and depth, like renaissance era paintings.

明暗对比：光与影之间的强烈对比，具有戏剧性和深度，就像文艺复兴时期的绘画一样。

![](img/bd985b7f70ca69fc712330e948f41576.png)

## Photogram: Shadows and shapes created by placing objectson photo paper.

照片：通过放置物体创建的阴影和形状在照片纸上。

![](img/0bc7390baa5a931df063f956ad2f50af.png)

## Distortion: Bending light for abstract or surreal effects.

扭曲：弯曲光线以获得抽象或超现实的效果。

![](img/7bafd5d5a78ebf4f1f50dcc16e90bfa0.png)

## Double exposure: Blending two images for a surreal effect.

双重曝光：混合两个图像以获得超现实的效果。

![](img/b671ea43a4eec90f7a5956aa095a5211.png)

## HDR: Wide range of tones, detailed highlights and shadows.

HDR：广泛的色调、详细的高光和阴影。

![](img/d447276aae638f800de4b14514670797.png)

## Juxtaposition of light and shadow: Play of light and dark for depth and drama.

光与影的并置：光与暗的游戏为了深度和戏剧性。

![](img/bb543ecc61af870a9d2af94f9ce7f505.png)

## Overexposed: Bright and minimal shadows, creating a positive and airy feel.

曝光过度：明亮且最小的阴影，营造积极、通风的感觉。

![](img/65cee12b2d49e1a873d9ec5d19c35fef.png)

## Silhouette: Dark subject against a light background, mystery, anonymity.

剪影：浅色背景下的黑暗主题，神秘，匿名。

![](img/10d0533b81c511a1e33daa77628e0cdd.png)

## Film noir: High contrast and dramatic shadows, like in old detective movies.

黑色电影：高对比度和戏剧性的阴影，就像老侦探电影里的那样。

![](img/02f64b9d60afe55a2fb77e49c24af037.png)
