
## Chapter 42: Linear Attention (RWKV) 第42章：线性注意力（RWKV）

Another modification to standard attention mechanisms, Ring Attention enables sub-quadratic full-context interaction via incremental computation with a “message-passing” structure, wherein “blocks” of context [communicate with each other](https://www.reddit.com/r/MachineLearning/comments/1arj2j8/d_gemini_1m10m_token_context_window_how/) over a series of steps rather than all at once. Within each block, the technique is essentially classical attention. While largely a research direction rather than standard technique at least within the open-weights world, Google’s Gemini is rumored to possibly be using Ring Attention in order to enable its million-plus-token context.

对标准注意力机制的另一种修改，环注意力通过增量计算与“消息传递”结构实现了次二次全上下文交互，其中上下文“块”通过一系列步骤而不是一次性全部相互通信。在每个块中，该技术本质上是经典的注意力。虽然至少在开放权重领域很大程度上是一个研究方向而不是标准技术，但有传言称谷歌的 Gemini 可能正在使用 Ring Attention 来实现其超过 100 万代币的上下文。

## Chapter 43: Structured State Space Models 第43章：结构化状态空间模型

The Receptance-Weighted Key Value (RWKV) architecture is a return to the general structure of RNN models (e.g LSTMs), with modifications to enable increased scaling and a linear attention-style mechanism which supports recurrent “unrolling” of its representation (allowing constant computation per output token as context length scales).

接收加权键值 (RWKV) 架构是对 RNN 模型（例如 LSTM）一般结构的回归，进行了修改以实现更大的扩展性和支持循环“展开”其表示的线性注意式机制（允许常数每个输出标记的计算作为上下文长度比例）。

## Chapter 45: Distribution Modeling 第45章：分布建模

Recalling our [first glimpse](https://mitesh1612.github.io/blog/2020/08/30/naive-bayes) of language models as simple bigram distributions, the most basic thing you can do in distributional modeling is just count co-occurrence probabilities in your dataset and repeat them as ground truth. This idea can be extended to conditional sampling or classification as “Naive Bayes” ([blog post](https://mitesh1612.github.io/blog/2020/08/30/naive-bayes), [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=O2L2Uv9pdDA)), often one of the simplest algorithms covered in introductory machine learning courses.

回想一下我们第一次看到的语言模型是简单的二元分布，在分布式建模中你可以做的最基本的事情就是计算数据集中的共现概率并将它们重复作为基本事实。这个想法可以扩展到条件采样或分类，如“朴素贝叶斯”（博客文章视频），通常是机器学习入门课程中介绍的最简单的算法之一。

The next generative model students are often taught is the Gaussian Mixture Model and its Expectation-Maximization algorithm; Gaussian Mixture Models + Expectation-Maximization algorithm. [This blog post](https://mpatacchiola.github.io/blog/2020/07/31/gaussian-mixture-models.html) and [this video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DODphRRL79c) give decent overviews; the core idea here is assuming that data distributions can be approximated as a mixture of multivariate Gaussian distributions. GMMs can also be used for clustering if individual groups can be assumed to be approximately Gaussian.

学生经常教授的下一个生成模型是高斯混合模型及其期望最大化算法；高斯混合模型+期望最大化算法。这篇博文和这段视频给出了不错的概述；这里的核心思想是假设数据分布可以近似为多元高斯分布的混合。如果可以假设各个组近似为高斯分布，则 GMM 也可以用于聚类。

While these methods aren’t very effective at representing complex structures like images or language, related ideas will appear as components of some of the more advanced methods we’ll see.

虽然这些方法在表示图像或语言等复杂结构方面并不是很有效，但相关的想法将作为我们将看到的一些更高级方法的组成部分出现。
