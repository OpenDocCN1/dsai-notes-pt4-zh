>
> Step 1: Create AI Avatar
>

>

>
> Step 2: Create Text-to-Audio
>
> Go to `http://beta.elevenlabs.io`
>
> Type in the text you want to convert to audio.

> Select the voice.

>
> Step 3: Create a Video
>
> Go to `http://studio.d-id.com`
>
> Create an account if you don't have one.
>
> Upload the avatar and audio file, then click on "Generate".
