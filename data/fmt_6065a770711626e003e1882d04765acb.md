
# 香港个人信息保护对儿童年龄的规定

> 来源：[https://docs.feishu.cn/docx/JyYzduNlSoU38PxFSIjc0X7QnNe](https://docs.feishu.cn/docx/JyYzduNlSoU38PxFSIjc0X7QnNe)

**-问**：有人知道香港个人信息方面对于儿童个人信息的保护，是否有明确的关于年龄的规定吗？

**-答1**：OneTrust DataGuidance里面有，是18岁。

> Where the data subject is a minor (i.e. under the age of 18), any prescribed consent required for using personal data for a new purpose can be given on his or her behalf by an individual who has parental responsibility for the minor (see DPP 3 and Section 2(1) of the PDPO).
