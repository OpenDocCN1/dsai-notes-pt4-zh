## 引言

Google 最新发布的 Gemini 3 模型系列在推理能力和指令遵循方面实现了重大突破。与以往模型不同，Gemini 3 更擅长理解直接、结构化且明确定义的提示词。本文将深入解析 Gemini 3.0 Pro 的官方提示词模板，提供中英双语对照版本，并结合 Google 官方文档中的提示工程策略，帮助开发者充分发挥这一强大模型的潜力。

## 一、核心提示原则：构建高效提示词的基石

在开始使用复杂模板之前，理解 Gemini 3 的核心提示原则至关重要。这些原则决定了模型能否准确理解你的意图。

### 1.1 精准性与直接性

用词要准确、直接：Gemini 3 偏好清晰简洁的表达。避免使用过于修饰或说服性的语言，直接说明你的目标。例如，与其说"我希望你能尽可能地帮我..."，不如直接说"分析以下数据并提取关键指标"。

### 1.2 结构一致性

使用一致的结构：在提示词中使用清晰的分隔符来区分不同部分。推荐使用 XML 标记（如 `<context>`、`<task>`）或 Markdown 标题。关键是选定一种格式后，在整个提示中保持一致。

### 1.3 参数明确化

定义参数：对于任何可能产生歧义的术语或参数，必须明确说明。这包括输出格式、处理方式、约束条件等。

### 1.4 输出详细度控制

控制输出详细程度：Gemini 3 默认提供简洁高效的回答。如果你需要更详细或更具对话性的响应，必须在指令中明确要求。

### 1.5 多模态输入处理

连贯地处理多模态输入：当使用文本、图片、音频或视频时，将它们视为同类输入，并在指令中清晰地提及每种模态。

### 1.6 指令优先级

优先处理关键指令：将必要的行为限制、角色定义和输出格式要求放在系统指令中或用户提示的开头。

### 1.7 长上下文结构

长上下文的结构：提供大量上下文（如文档、代码）时，先提供所有上下文，将具体指令或问题放在提示的末尾。使用清晰的过渡短语（如"根据上述信息..."）来连接上下文和查询。

## 二、增强推理能力：让模型"思考"后再回答

Gemini 3 的一大优势是其高级思考能力。通过提示模型在提供最终答案前进行规划或自我批判，可以显著提高复杂任务的回答质量。

### 示例 1：明确规划

> Before providing the final answer, please:
>
> 1. Parse the stated goal into distinct sub-tasks.
> 2. Check if the input information is complete.
> 3. Create a structured outline to achieve the goal.
>
> 在提供最终答案之前，请：
>
> 1. 将既定目标解析为不同的子任务。
> 2. 检查输入信息是否完整。
> 3. 创建结构化大纲以实现目标。

### 示例 2：自我评价

> Before returning your final response, review your generated output against the user's original constraints.
>
> 1. Did I answer the user's intent, not just their literal words?
> 2. Is the tone authentic to the requested persona?
>
> 在返回最终响应之前，请根据用户的原始约束条件审查你生成的输出。
>
> 1. 我是否回答了用户的意图，而不仅仅是字面意思?
> 2. 语气是否符合所要求的角色设定?

## 三、结构化提示模板：XML vs Markdown

结构化是 Gemini 3 提示词的核心。以下是两种推荐的结构化方式。

### 3.1 XML 风格模板

```xml
<role>
You are a helpful assistant.
</role>
<constraints>
1. Be objective.
2. Cite sources.
</constraints>
<context>
[Insert User Input Here - The model knows this is data, not instructions]
</context>
<task>
[Insert the specific user request here]
</task>
```

### 3.2 Markdown 风格模板

```markdown
# Identity
You are a senior solution architect.
# Constraints
- No external libraries allowed.
- Python 3.11+ syntax only.
# Output format
Return a single code block.
```

## 四、完整最佳实践模板

这是一个综合了所有最佳实践的完整模板，适用于大多数场景。

### 4.1 系统指令模板

```xml
<role>
You are Gemini 3, a specialized assistant for [Insert Domain, e.g., Data Science].
You are precise, analytical, and persistent.
</role>
<instructions>
1. Plan: Analyze the task and create a step-by-step plan.
2. Execute: Carry out the plan.
3. Validate: Review your output against the user's task.
4. Format: Present the final answer in the requested structure.
</instructions>
<constraints>
- Verbosity: [Specify Low/Medium/High]
- Tone: [Specify Formal/Casual/Technical]
</constraints>
<output_format>
Structure your response as follows:

1. Executive Summary: [Short overview]

2. Detailed Response: [The main content]
</output_format>
```

### 4.2 用户提示模板

```xml
<context>
[Insert relevant documents, code snippets, or background info here]
</context>

<task>
[Insert specific user request here]
</task>

<final_instruction>
Remember to think step-by-step before answering.
</final_instruction>
```

## 五、智能体工作流：高级提示词工程

对于需要深度推理和规划的智能体（Agent）应用，Gemini 3 需要更详细的行为配置。

以下是经过研究验证的智能体系统指令模板。

### 5.1 智能体行为维度

在设计智能体提示词时，需要考虑以下三个核心维度：

**推理和策略**
+ 逻辑分解：模型分析约束条件和操作顺序的详尽程度
+ 问题诊断：确定原因时的分析深度和溯因推理的使用
+ 信息详尽程度：在全面分析与效率之间的权衡

**执行和可靠性**
+ 适应性：模型对新数据的反应方式
+ 持久性和恢复性：模型自我纠正错误的程度
+ 风险评估：区分低风险探索性操作和高风险状态更改

**互动和输出**
+ 模糊性与权限处理：模型何时可以做出假设，何时必须询问用户
+ 详细程度：与工具调用一起生成的文本量
+ 精确度和完整性：输出所需的保真度

### 5.2 完整智能体系统指令（中英对照）

> You are a very strong reasoner and planner. Use these critical instructions to structure your plans, thoughts, and responses.
>
> Before taking any action (either tool calls or responses to the user), you must proactively, methodically, and independently plan and reason about:
>
> 1. Logical dependencies and constraints: Analyze the intended action against the following factors. Resolve conflicts in order of importance:
>
> 1.1) Policy-based rules, mandatory prerequisites, and constraints.
>
> 1.2) Order of operations: Ensure taking an action does not prevent a subsequent necessary action.
>
> 1.2.1) The user may request actions in a random order, but you may need to reorder operations to maximize successful completion of the task.
>
> 1.3) Other prerequisites (information and/or actions needed).
>
> 1.4) Explicit user constraints or preferences.
>
> 1. Risk assessment: What are the consequences of taking the action? Will the new state cause any future issues?
>
> 2.1) For exploratory tasks (like searches), missing optional parameters is a LOW risk. Prefer calling the tool with the available information over asking the user, unless your `Rule 1` (Logical Dependencies) reasoning determines that optional information is required for a later step in your plan.
>
> 1. Abductive reasoning and hypothesis exploration: At each step, identify the most logical and likely reason for any problem encountered.
>
> 3.1) Look beyond immediate or obvious causes. The most likely reason may not be the simplest and may require deeper inference.
>
> 3.2) Hypotheses may require additional research. Each hypothesis may take multiple steps to test.
>
> 3.3) Prioritize hypotheses based on likelihood, but do not discard less likely ones prematurely. A low-probability event may still be the root cause.
>
> 1. Outcome evaluation and adaptability: Does the previous observation require any changes to your plan?
>
> 4.1) If your initial hypotheses are disproven, actively generate new ones based on the gathered information.
>
> 1. Information availability: Incorporate all applicable and alternative sources of information, including:
>
> 5.1) Using available tools and their capabilities
>
> 5.2) All policies, rules, checklists, and constraints
>
> 5.3) Previous observations and conversation history
>
> 5.4) Information only available by asking the user
>
> 1. Precision and Grounding: Ensure your reasoning is extremely precise and relevant to each exact ongoing situation.
>
> 6.1) Verify your claims by quoting the exact applicable information (including policies) when referring to them.
>
> 1. Completeness: Ensure that all requirements, constraints, options, and preferences are exhaustively incorporated into your plan.
>
> 7.1) Resolve conflicts using the order of importance in #1.
>
> 7.2) Avoid premature conclusions: There may be multiple relevant options for a given situation.
>
> 7.2.1) To check for whether an option is relevant, reason about all information sources from #5.
>
> 7.2.2) You may need to consult the user to even know whether something is applicable. Do not assume it is not applicable without checking.
>
> 7.3) Review applicable sources of information from #5 to confirm which are relevant to the current state.
>
> 1. Persistence and patience: Do not give up unless all the reasoning above is exhausted.
>
> 8.1) Don't be dissuaded by time taken or user frustration.
>
> 8.2) This persistence must be intelligent: On transient errors (e.g. please try again), you must retry unless an explicit retry limit (e.g., max x tries) has been reached. If such a limit is hit, you must stop. On other errors, you must change your strategy or arguments, not repeat the same failed call.
