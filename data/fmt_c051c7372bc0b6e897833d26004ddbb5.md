## Quantum Brilliance Quoll - 钻石量子微处理器

![](img/63ea07b8accaaaa4d24bbd4e42818c1d.png)

> Most modern quantum computers need to be cooled and vacuum-sealed to protect qubits with special properties. At Oak Ridge National Lab (ORNL) in Tennessee, Quantum Brilliance recently integrated the facility’s first onsite, commercial quantum-classical hybrid cluster that uses naturally occurring defects in diamonds as qubits, says CEO Marcus Doherty. The diamonds are hardier than traditional qubits, and can operate at room temperature, saving energy and space. The quantum microprocessor, which is about the size of a desktop computer and could help simulate chemistry between molecules, preserved quantum states for over a millisecond in tests.

> 大多数现代量子计算机需要冷却和真空密封，以保护具有特殊属性的量子比特。位于田纳西州橡树岭国家实验室 (ORNL) 的 Quantum Brilliance 公司首席执行官马库斯·多赫蒂 (Marcus Doherty) 表示，该公司最近集成了该设施首个现场商用量子-经典混合集群，该集群利用钻石中天然存在的缺陷作为量子比特。这些钻石比传统量子比特更坚硬，并且可以在室温下运行，从而节省能源和空间。该量子微处理器大小与台式计算机相当，可以帮助模拟分子间的化学反应，在测试中将量子态保持了超过一毫秒。

## XRlabs SIM - 练习手术

> When neurosurgeon Ali Rezaei Haddad learned in 2003 that Iranian conjoined twins had died during separation surgery, he vowed to build technology that could prevent such tragedies. Two decades later, his London-based company has developed XRlabs SIM, which uses AI to transform ordinary medical scans into immersive 3D environments where surgeons can rehearse life-or-death procedures. Last year the technology enabled the successful separation of conjoined Pakistani twins Minal and Mirha, allowing doctors to explore the 11-month-olds’ shared anatomy in advance and practice the 14-hour surgery. The mixed-reality platform has been used by doctors in the U.K., U.S. and Turkey.

> 2003 年，神经外科医生阿里·雷扎伊·哈达德（Ali Rezaei Haddad）得知伊朗一对连体双胞胎在分离手术中死亡后，立志要研发能够防止此类悲剧重演的技术。二十年后，他位于伦敦的公司开发了 XRlabs SIM，利用人工智能将普通的医学扫描图像转换为沉浸式 3D 环境，外科医生可以在其中演练生死攸关的手术。去年，这项技术成功分离了巴基斯坦连体双胞胎米纳尔（Minal）和米尔哈（Mirha），医生们得以提前探索这对 11 个月大婴儿的共同解剖结构，并练习这场长达 14 小时的手术。该混合现实平台已被英国、美国和土耳其的医生使用。
