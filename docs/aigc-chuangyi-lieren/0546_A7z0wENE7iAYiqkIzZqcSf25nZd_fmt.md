
# Nano Banana 提示词精选

> 来源：[https://aigcreative.feishu.cn/docx/NTuddYxLIorEbkxSdiccWIvcnVf](https://aigcreative.feishu.cn/docx/NTuddYxLIorEbkxSdiccWIvcnVf)

最近这段时间谷歌Nano Banana太火了，基本上改变了图像编辑领域的范式，但是很多小伙伴不知道有哪些比较好用的提示词。

下面是收集到的比较推荐的提示词，主要是英文，大家也可以结合翻译工具换成中文。

## 一）分镜 / 角色一致性（Storyboard）

适合“上传人物/虚拟角色 → 直接出分镜图”。

1. Turn the uploaded portrait into a collectible **character figure**. Place a branded box behind it (box shows the character image), a computer screen on the side showing a Blender modeling viewport, and a round plastic base under the figure. **PVC look**, indoor studio, **realistic lighting, film tone**, keep face identity.

2. Keep the same character. Scene 1: **subway platform**, cool blue ambience, rim light from behind; camera at chest height, medium shot.

3. Same character, **coffee shop close-up**, shallow depth-of-field (bokeh), warm golden hour light, hands holding a paper cup with subtle steam, keep identity and hairstyle.

4. Same character, **office night scene**, glass reflections, city lights bokeh, three-point lighting, medium-wide.

5. Same character, warehouse with volumetric light rays through windows; dust motes, slightly desaturated cinematic look.

6. Storyboard 6 frames: hero intro, establishing shot, close-up on hands, over-the-shoulder UI shot, product hero, end card. Keep character identity across frames, consistent wardrobe.

7. Turn pose to a walking forward action, slight motion blur on legs, maintain facial details.

8. Same character in **rainy street**, wet asphalt reflections, neon signs; keep eye color and freckles.

9. Same character, studio packshot background in neutral gray; soft top light, fill card from camera left; expressions neutral.

10. Generate a shot list of 8 frames for an unboxing: box on table → hands open → figure removed → detail close-ups → turntable shot → closing hero. Keep product scale and branding.

## 二）电商 / 产品合成（商品+场景融合）

1. Merge `image1` (product) with `image2` (modern kitchen). Place the product on a **marble countertop**, keep realistic reflections and shadows; color temperature ~5000K, slight vignette.

2. Cosmetics bottle on **acrylic pedestal**, seamless paper backdrop, softbox from right, subtle gradient background, specular highlights controlled.

3. Sneakers on **concrete block**, low-angle hero shot, studio rim light, faint haze for depth.

4. Smartphone **floating exploded view**: separated layers for screen, mid-frame, camera module; dark studio, glossy reflections managed.

5. Watch macro shot, **90° top-down**, soft diffused light, brushed metal texture visible, anti-alias text on dial.

6. A/B variants: same layout, change only background material (wood / stainless / terrazzo), keep product color constant.

7. Lifestyle placement: living room sofa, daylight from window camera left, product on side-table; white balance matched to scene.

8. Promo trio: front / 45° / top angles in one session; consistent brand palette and shadows.

## 三）人像妆造 / 服饰替换（注意保持身份一致）

1. Keep face identity; change hairstyle to **retro bob**, add thin eyeliner, soft skin retouch while preserving pores, realistic hair strand flyaways.

2. Outfit swap to **streetwear (oversized hoodie + cargo pants)**, neutral studio background, soft Rembrandt pattern lighting.

3. Add sunglasses with subtle reflections, ensure eyes still faintly visible through lens tint.

4. Formal portrait, **key light 45°**, fill −1EV, background seamless #EAEAEA, color-accurate skin tone.

5. Outdoor candid, overcast sky as giant softbox, slight film grain, maintain freckles and moles.

6. Headshot for ID: straight-on camera, no smiles, flat even light, neutral background, maintain exact facial geometry.

## 四）光影与摄影语汇（直接“像摄影师”说话）

1. Film noir: **hard key from high angle**, deep shadows, practical lamp in background, smoke for volume.

2. Golden hour backlight, **rim light on hair**, flare streaks, warm highlights ~5600K.

3. Top-down softbox with negative fill on both sides to shape the face, subtle catchlight.

4. High-key beauty, near-white background, contrast low, speculars controlled.

5. Rain reflections, **wet-down street**, neon glow; long-exposure light trails.

6. Product hero: **flagged highlights**, gradient card for polished metal, reflection on base kept tight.

## 五）材质与质感（让“质地”更可信）

1. PVC plastic look, slightly translucent edges, smooth specular roll-off, no micro-scratches.

2. Anodized aluminum, fine linear brushing, soft anisotropic highlights.

3. Matte ceramic, soft diffuse, micro-pitted texture visible only in macro.

4. Brushed stainless steel with subtle fingerprints near button, realistic.

5. Frosted glass bottle, internal light scatter, bright meniscus line on liquid.

## 六）文字 / 海报（含文本渲染）

官方强调对**包含文字**的图像有针对性优化，适合海报/Logo/封面。(Google Developers Blog)

1. Poster: bold headline “AI VIDEO STUDIO”, sub-headline small caps, clean sans serif, centered layout; hero product bottom-right, strong grid, optical margin alignment.

2. Minimalist **event flyer**: date big numbers “09·10”, venue in small line below; keep text crisp and aligned.

3. App mock poster: phone frame with screen UI, headline 5-6 words, badge “NEW”.

4. Brand logo lockup on dark background, subtle glow, high legibility, no distortions.

5. YouTube thumbnail style: big face close-up + 3-5 word punchy title; high contrast; rule of thirds.

## 七）合成 / 场景替换（两图融合与换景）

**官方/媒体均提到**多步编辑与两图融合是亮点；注意让光源/焦距/色温匹配以更写实。(Axios, Google AI Studio, Reddit)

1. Replace background with snowy mountain scene; match key light from camera right; footprints faint in snow.

2. Place subject into **museum gallery**, polished floor reflections, soft track lighting from ceiling.

3. Combine portrait with **night city rooftop**; wind-blown hair; cool key, warm practicals behind.

4. Merge product with **bathroom marble vanity**; soft window light, mirror reflection consistent.

5. Subject into **library**, warm tungsten practicals; lens fall-off consistent with 50mm look.

6. Remove clutter from desk; keep laptop and mug; fill missing areas with plausible textures.

## 八）镜头语言 / 构图（给“画面调度”可控性）

1. 35mm equivalent, eye-level, medium shot, rule of thirds.

2. 85mm portrait, shallow DOF, background separation.

3. Top-down **flat-lay**, grid-aligned objects, neat spacing.

4. Low-angle hero shot, leading lines converge to subject.

5. Tilt-shift miniature look, thin plane of focus through subject.

## 九）特效 / 情绪氛围

1. Light fog + god rays from window; particles visible only against backlight.

2. Subtle film grain (fine), chromatic aberration minimal, letterboxed.

3. Rain streaks + surface ripples on puddles, reflections intact.

4. Fireflies/bokeh spheres floating, depth-based size variation.

5. Confetti burst, shallow DOF; motion blur only on confetti.

## 十）多轮对话脚本

多轮迭代是官方推荐的最佳实践。(Google Developers Blog)

脚本 A｜人物置景分镜（3 步）

+ 第 1 轮：Upload the portrait. Place the same person in a sunlit café terrace; warm golden hour, shallow DOF, cinematic.

+ 第 2 轮：Keep identity. Add a laptop with code editor on screen; add a cup with visible steam; adjust white balance slightly cooler.

+ 第 3 轮：Tighten composition to medium shot; brighten catchlights; add gentle film grain.

脚本 B｜商品三视图（3 步）

+ 第 1 轮：Front hero shot on neutral backdrop; soft top light, clean shadows.

+ 第 2 轮：Add 45° view; match exposure and shadow direction.

+ 第 3 轮：Add top view; assemble a 3-panel layout with consistent margins.

脚本 C｜角色手办化（3 步）

+ 第 1 轮：Turn subject into a PVC figure on round base; indoor studio, film tone.

+ 第 2 轮：Add branded box behind + monitor showing Blender modeling.

+ 第 3 轮：Refine edges on hair and fingers; ensure specular highlights are smooth.

## 实操要点（精炼）

+ 用段落描述场景，不要只堆关键词；先大结构，再补光影/材质细节（官方写法建议）。(Google AI for Developers)



# 知识库动态

+ 多轮微调（光源方向、景深、裁边、高光控制），每次只改 1–2 个因素（官方推荐）。(Google Developers Blog)

+ 角色一致性：上传清晰参考图；固定发型/肤色/面部细节用语，减少大幅度冲突改动（场景/光源匹配更真实）。(Google AI Studio, Reddit)

+ 限制认知：有些基础编辑（如精准裁剪到特定比例）体验一般，可用“生成时就指定构图+留白”来规避。(PC Gamer)
