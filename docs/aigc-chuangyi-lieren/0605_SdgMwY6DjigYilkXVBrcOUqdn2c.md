## Split lighting: One half of the subject is lit, while the other half is in shadow, creating a dramatic effect.

分光照明：主体的一半被照亮，而另一半则处于阴影中，创造出戏剧性的效果。

![](img/45ec3ae26ef590b4b8a57d0a461d387f.png)

## Cinematic lighting: Strong contrasts, shadows, and dramatic color palettes to evoke emotions and tell stories.

电影灯光：强烈的对比、阴影和戏剧性的调色板可以唤起情感并讲述故事。

![](img/11af9029bdadfad4b2fd095849cc8402.png)

## Studio lighting: Your image looks like it was taken in a professional studio with great lighting and shot with a high-quality camera.

工作室照明：您的图像看起来像是在专业工作室拍摄的，光线充足，并使用高品质相机拍摄。

![](img/cacec9d6fe853b13435435f486c38482.png)

## Rim lighting: Light outlines the subject's edges, separating them from the background and adding depth.

边缘照明：光线勾勒出拍摄对象的边缘，将其与背景分开并增加深度。

![](img/5a9a1191dc1611bccf662337e9a21d8b.png)

## Volumetric lighting: Adds depth and atmosphere to the scene by showcasing how light moves through the air, often highlighting dust particles or mist.

体积照明：通过展示光线如何在空气中移动，通常突出灰尘颗粒或薄雾，为场景增添深度和氛围。

![](img/62a2c1ff0ea56ba9d473599cd608af87.png)

## Stage lighting: Uses spotlights, colored gels, and other specialized equipment to create dynamic and engaging visuals.

舞台灯光：使用聚光灯、彩色凝胶和其他专用设备来创造动态且引人入胜的视觉效果。

![](img/54d91c4abf680254ed377b6b4ba8a786.png)

## Spotlight: Focused beams of light to highlight specific areas.

聚光灯：聚焦光束以突出显示特定区域。

![](img/d4bdbfc092b74464da42d445ab9787c5.png)

## Neon lights: Bright and colorful, perfect for adding a modern or retro vibe.

霓虹灯：明亮多彩，非常适合增添现代或复古氛围。
