2024年3月19日，由DJI大疆旗下专业航拍及影像社交平台SkyPixel天空之城™举办的天空之城9周年影像大赛，于今日落下帷幕。来自美国的摄影师Luke Bredar的作品《Haines Alaska Through the Eyes of Luke Bredar》斩获年度最佳视频大奖；中国摄影师道来的作品《蒙古包》摘得年度最佳图片大奖的桂冠。

![](img/25a81270c94d3c2aeb9038f3f782a74e.png)

# 年度大奖

> 摄影师：道来

> 作品名：《蒙古包》

> 简介：在中国内蒙古自治区赤峰市克什克腾旗乌兰布统，这是一个雪后的早晨，牧民们围绕着蒙古包，为了即将开始的草原冰雪那达慕活动，各自做着准备工作。 当时的场景非常有画面感，也有生活气息，自然而生动，围绕着蒙古包，牧民、马队、骆驼、敖包……一切都仿佛置身于中国传统水墨画里一般！ 雪景是拍摄水墨意境的绝佳机会，由于雪的亮白导致画面呈现出黑白水墨的效果，同时由于雪的遮盖，画面更干净、更简洁，留白、线条、勾勒、皴染……中国画的传统笔触都能自然呈现出来，甚至于都不需要做过多的后期调整。 大疆御3Pro无人机恰好是拍摄这类题材的绝好帮手，我在远处，飞起了无人机，为了不打扰到被拍摄对象，使用了三倍变焦，相当于70mm焦段，对景深有一定的压缩，让画面层次更紧密，4800万像素确保了画面的宽度和画质，足以表现和记录整个现场的情况。

# 第三名

> 摄影师：

> 作品名：《Bird's eye view on Iceland》

> 简介：The common subject of this photo series is Iceland from the bird's eye perspective. Iceland is a land of unparalleled natural beauty, a place where rugged landscapes, volcanic terrain, and pristine wilderness converge in a symphony of breathtaking vistas. For drone photographers, this Nordic island is nothing short of a dream canvas, offering the opportunity to capture its wonders from a unique and mesmerizing perspective. Iceland's diverse terrain is a visual masterpiece when seen from above. The ability to launch a drone into the skies allows photographers to capture the country's iconic sights like colorful volcanos, meandering rivers, glacial formations, and geothermal wonders from angles previously unattainable. Iceland is renowned for its remote and hard-to-reach destinations. Drones provide access to these hidden gems without disturbing the fragile ecosystems or risking personal safety. Stunning locations like the Highlands, remote coastal cliffs, and glacier rivers are now within reach for photographers. The moment my drone took off I was transported to another world. From high above, you can truly appreciate the geological wonders that define this island.

![](img/e0b4dec09fd3239bd5cc7b4727ad22de.png)

# 第五名

> 摄影师：ekrem_sahin07

> 作品名：《EYE IN THE LAKE》

> 简介：While the drought in the summer months reduces the water level in the lake, birds and other creatures living in the lake are having a hard time. Especially the habitats of migratory birds are disappearing day by day. They walk for hours in the hope of finding food and nutrients in the lake, which is starting to dry up. The shapes they create are interesting. This image captured in Yarisli Lake Burdur Türkiye The existence of the world and the protection of water, which is the only source of all living things in the past and today, is a global problem. As a photographer, I closely follow the water problem, which has been felt very much in the world and in Turkey lately. With visual language, I want to show that in the depths of these cosmic images lies the figure of a tree, an unborn child, sometimes a flower seed. I want to present the pain in the veins of nature with an aesthetic approach. PROTECT THE WORLD
