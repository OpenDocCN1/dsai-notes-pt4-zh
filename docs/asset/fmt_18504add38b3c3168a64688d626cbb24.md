
[Reinforcement Learning (`RL`)](https://genai-handbook.github.io/#finetuning-methods-for-llms) will come up most directly when we look at finetuning methods in Section IV, and may also be a useful mental model for thinking about [“`agent`”](https://genai-handbook.github.io/#tool-use-and-agents) applications and some of the “control theory” notions which come up for state-space models. Like a lot of the topics discussed in this document, you can go quite deep down many different `RL`-related threads if you’d like; as it relates to language modeling and alignment, it’ll be most important to be comfortable with the basic problem setup for Markov decision processes, notion of policies and trajectories, and high-level understanding of standard iterative + gradient-based optimization methods for `RL`.

当我们在第四节中查看微调方法时，强化学习（`RL`）将最直接地出现，并且也可能是思考“代理”应用程序和状态空间模型中出现的一些“控制理论”概念的有用心智模型。与本文档中讨论的许多主题一样，如果您愿意，可以深入研究许多不同的 `RL` 相关主题；因为它涉及语言建模和对齐，所以最重要的是熟悉马尔可夫决策过程的基本问题设置、策略和轨迹的概念，以及对 `RL` 标准迭代 + 基于梯度的优化方法的高级理解。

This [blog post](https://lilianweng.github.io/posts/2018-02-19-rl-overview/) from Lilian Weng is a great starting point, and is quite dense with important `RL` ideas despite its relative conciseness. It also touches on connections to `AlphaGo` and gameplay, which you might find interesting as well.

Lilian Weng 的这篇博文是一个很好的起点，尽管相对简洁，但其中包含了重要的 `RL` 思想。它还涉及与 `AlphaGo` 和游戏玩法的联系，您可能也会觉得有趣。

The textbook [“Reinforcement Learning: An Introduction”](https://web.stanford.edu/class/psych209/Readings/SuttonBartoIPRLBook2ndEd.pdf) by Sutton and Barto is generally considered the classic reference text for the area, at least for “non-deep” methods. This was my primary guide when I was first learning about `RL`, and it gives a more in-depth exploration of many of the topics touched on in Lilian’s blog post.

Sutton 和 Barto 的教科书《强化学习：简介》通常被认为是该领域的经典参考书，至少对于“非深度”方法而言是如此。这是我第一次学习 `RL` 时的主要指南，它对 Lilian 博客文章中涉及的许多主题进行了更深入的探索。

If you want to jump ahead to some more neural-flavored content, [Andrej Karpathy](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VMj-3S1tku0&list=PLAqhIrjkxbuWI23v9cThsA9GvCAUhRvKZ) has a nice blog post on deep `RL`; [this manuscript](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1810.06339) by Yuxi Li and [this textbook](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2201.02135) by Aske Plaat may be useful for further deep dives.

## Chapter 6: Markov Models 第 6 章：马尔可夫模型

Running a fixed policy in a Markov decision process yields a Markov chain; processes resembling this kind of setup are fairly abundant, and many branches of machine learning involve modeling systems under Markovian assumptions (i.e. lack of path-dependence, given the current state). This [blog post](https://thagomizer.com/blog/2017/11/07/markov-models.html) from Aja Hammerly makes a nice case for thinking about language models via Markov processes, and this [post](https://ericmjl.github.io/essays-on-data-science/machine-learning/markov-models/) from “Essays on Data Science” has examples and code building up towards auto-regressive Hidden Markov Models, which will start to vaguely resemble some of the neural network architectures we’ll look at later on.

在马尔可夫决策过程中运行固定策略会产生马尔可夫链；类似这种设置的过程相当丰富，并且机器学习的许多分支都涉及马尔可夫假设下的建模系统（即，在给定当前状态的情况下，缺乏路径依赖性）。 Aja Hammerly 的这篇博客文章为通过马尔可夫过程思考语言模型提供了一个很好的案例，这篇来自“数据科学论文”的文章提供了构建自回归隐马尔可夫模型的示例和代码，这将开始隐约类似于一些我们稍后将讨论神经网络架构。

This [blog post](https://www.tweag.io/blog/2019-10-25-mcmc-intro1/) from Simeon Carstens gives a nice coverage of Markov chain Monte Carlo methods, which are powerful and widely-used techniques for sampling from implicitly-represented distributions, and are helpful for thinking about probabilistic topics ranging from stochastic gradient descent to diffusion.

Simeon Carstens 的这篇博文很好地介绍了马尔可夫链蒙特卡罗方法，这是一种强大且广泛使用的从隐式表示的分布中进行采样的技术，有助于思考从随机梯度下降到扩散等概率主题。

[Markov models](https://mlg.eng.cam.ac.uk/zoubin/papers/ijprai.pdf) are also at the heart of many Bayesian methods. See this tutorial [from Zoubin Ghahramani](https://www.deeplearningbook.org/contents/graphical_models.html) for a nice overview, the textbook [“Pattern Recognition and Machine Learning”](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/uploads/prod/2006/01/Bishop-Pattern-Recognition-and-Machine-Learning-2006.pdf) for Bayesian angles on many machine learning topics (as well as a more-involved HMM presentation), and this chapter of the Goodfellow et al. “Deep Learning” textbook for some connections to deep learning.

马尔可夫模型也是许多贝叶斯方法的核心。请参阅 Zoubin Ghahramani 的本教程以获取很好的概述、有关许多机器学习主题的贝叶斯角度的教科书“模式识别和机器学习”（以及更多涉及的 HMM 演示文稿）以及 Goodfellow 等人的本章。“深度学习”教科书，介绍与深度学习的一些联系。
