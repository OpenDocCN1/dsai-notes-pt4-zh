## For consultants

> Create an ambulatory care expansion plan for a health system evaluating 5 suburban locations: demographic heat mapping, service line priorities (urgent care, imaging), site selection scoring, partnership vs. build analysis, capital requirements ($12M), patient volume projections, and breakeven timeline (18 months)
> 为一家医疗系统制定一项门诊服务扩展计划，评估5个郊区地点：人口热力图、服务线优先级（紧急护理、影像检查）、选址评分、合作与自建分析、资金需求（1200万美元）、患者数量预测以及盈亏平衡时间表（18个月）

> Build a pharmaceutical launch strategy for a new diabetes medication: market landscape (3 established competitors), target prescriber segments (endocrinologists, PCPs), payer access plan, sales force sizing (120 reps), medical education program, launch timeline, and Year 1 revenue forecast ($85M)
> 为一款新的糖尿病药物制定药品上市策略：市场格局（3家成熟竞争对手）、目标处方医生细分群体（内分泌科医生、初级保健医生）、支付方准入计划、销售团队规模（120名代表）、医学教育项目、上市时间表以及第一年收入预测（8500万美元）

> Design a clinical trial optimization plan for a biotech's Phase III oncology study: current enrollment challenges (12 sites, 60% of target), site selection criteria, patient recruitment strategy, digital tools to reduce dropouts, timeline acceleration, and projected $2M savings
> 为一家生物技术公司的III期肿瘤学研究设计一项临床试验优化计划：当前的入组挑战（12个研究点，完成目标的60%）、研究点选择标准、患者招募策略、减少患者退出的数字工具、加速时间表，以及预计节省200万美元

> Create a portfolio optimization strategy for a regional REIT managing 45 commercial properties: asset performance review, hold vs. sell recommendations, underperforming strip mall disposition plan, capital reallocation to multifamily, and 5-year value creation roadmap
> 为管理45处商业地产的区域REIT制定投资组合优化策略：资产绩效评估、持有与出售建议、表现不佳的商业街购物中心处置计划、资本重新配置到多户住宅，以及5年价值创造路线图

> Build a market feasibility study for a 200-unit mixed-use development in downtown Austin: demographic analysis, comparable projects, demand projections, absorption rates, construction costs, financing options, timeline, and expected 18% IRR
> 为奥斯汀市中心一个200单元的综合用途开发项目进行市场可行性研究：人口统计分析、可比项目、需求预测、吸纳率、建设成本、融资方案、时间表以及预期18%的内部收益率

> Build a digital transformation roadmap for your insurance company's board: current maturity (mostly paper-based), industry tech trends, top 5 initiatives, customer portal plans, data modernization, 3-year timeline, and business outcomes
> 为您的保险公司董事会制定数字化转型路线图：当前成熟度（主要为纸质化）、行业技术趋势、前五项举措、客户门户计划、数据现代化、三年时间表以及业务成果

> Design a pricing strategy for your B2B software company's revenue team: current model analysis, competitor benchmarks, customer research findings, new tiered structure (Starter/Pro/Enterprise), revenue impact (+25%), and rollout plan
> 为您的B2B软件公司的营收团队设计定价策略：当前模式分析、竞争对手对标、客户研究结果、新的分层结构（入门级/专业版/企业版）、营收影响（+25%）和推出计划

> Create a customer experience strategy for your hotel chain's CMO: journey map with pain points (slow check-in), guest feedback themes, CX maturity (level 2 of 5), improvements, tech needed, and NPS lift from 45 to 65
> 为您连锁酒店的CMO制定客户体验策略：包含痛点（入住速度慢）的旅程地图、宾客反馈主题、客户体验成熟度（五级中的二级）、改进措施、所需技术，以及将NPS从45提升至65

> Build a due diligence presentation for your investment committee evaluating a fintech acquisition: their business model, market position (#3 in payments), financials, technology stack, management team, synergies, risks, and $50M valuation
> 为你的投资委员会制作一份尽职调查演示文稿，评估一项金融科技收购：包括其商业模式、市场地位（支付领域排名第三）、财务状况、技术栈、管理团队、协同效应、风险以及5000万美元的估值

> Design an operational efficiency review for a logistics company's COO: warehouse process analysis, industry benchmarks (you're 20% slower), automation opportunities, redesigned workflow, implementation plan, and projected $500K savings
> 为一家物流公司的首席运营官（COO）设计运营效率审查：仓库流程分析、行业基准（贵公司慢20%）、自动化机会、重新设计的工作流程、实施计划以及预计节省50万美元

> Create a competitive analysis for your e-commerce platform's leadership: map top 5 competitors by features vs. price, market share breakdown, your differentiation strategy (same-day delivery + sustainability), and win-loss patterns

> Build a consulting proposal for a manufacturing company evaluating supply chain optimization

> Design a market entry strategy for your SaaS company evaluating Latin America expansion: market size (5M potential customers), competitive landscape, customer segments, launch approach, operations needed, financial projections, and risks
> 为您的SaaS公司评估拉美扩张制定市场进入策略：市场规模（500万潜在客户）、竞争格局、客户细分、上市方法、所需运营、财务预测以及风险

> Create an organizational transformation presentation for your retail bank's executives: vision for digital-first banking, current vs. future state, roadmap with quick wins (mobile deposits in Q1), and how success gets measured

> Build a technology implementation proposal for your healthcare network's leadership: current infrastructure gaps, proposed cloud migration plan, patient care improvements, 18-month rollout, $2M budget, and 3-year ROI projections

> Build a risk analysis for a restaurant expansion plotting labor shortages (likely/high impact) vs. supplier issues (unlikely/high impact)

> Create a digital maturity assessment for a B2B distributor at Level 2 (basic website) with a roadmap to Level 4 (e-commerce, CRM)

> Present HR consulting in three tiers: Essential (compliance), Growth (talent strategy), Partner (embedded leadership)

> Build a change plan for a financial services firm moving 500 employees to hybrid work—covering communication, training, and reinforcement

> Build a discovery presentation for a manufacturing client sorting whether supply chain delays are process issues or capacity problems
