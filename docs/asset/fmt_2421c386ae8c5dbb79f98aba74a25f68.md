
> Each year around the world, 500,000 fetal deaths occur due to preeclampsia, a condition that causes high blood pressure, endangering the mother as well. Previously, doctors had no way of identifying high-risk moms before they showed symptoms, but a new test called Encompass changes that. It’s the first diagnostic to use cell-free RNA in blood to predict complications four months in advance. In a 10,000-patient study published this spring, Encompass correctly identified 91% of women who would develop preterm preeclampsia. Launched in May, Encompass is now available through physician practices like Advantia Health and partnerships like WIN.

> 全球每年有 50 万例胎儿死于先兆子痫。先兆子痫会导致高血压，并危及孕妇的生命。此前，医生无法在高危孕妇出现症状之前识别她们，但一项名为 Encompass 的新检测改变了这一现状。它是首个利用血液中的游离 RNA 提前四个月预测并发症的诊断方法。在今年春季发表的一项涉及 1 万名患者的研究中，Encompass 正确识别了 91%可能患上早产先兆子痫的女性。Encompass 于今年 5 月推出，目前可通过 Advantia Health 等医疗机构以及 WIN 等合作伙伴获得。

## Njembuma Company OBM-Drape - 挽救分娩生命

![](img/06140accfa722363f177c49cbee20891.png)

> Severe bleeding after childbirth can be life-threatening. Nairobi-based Njembuma Company developed the Objective Blood Measurement (OBM)-Drape to detect heavy bleeding early. This year, the company plans to increase production from 300,000 to 2 million drapes, and they’re now available across Africa and Asia.

> 产后严重出血可能危及生命。总部位于内罗毕的恩杰布马公司（Njembuma Company）开发了客观血量测量（OBM）巾，用于及早发现大量出血。运营总监西蒙·马伊纳（Simon Maina）表示，这种可生物降解的装置放置在产床上，用于收集和测量分娩过程中的失血量，以便医护人员在失血量达到一定水平时进行干预。2023 年的一项研究发现，采血巾可降低分娩并发症和死亡的风险。今年，该公司计划将采血巾的产量从 30 万片增至 200 万片，目前已在非洲和亚洲各地销售。

## Proprio Paradigm - 为外科医生制作实时地图

![](img/7ade3300680b1d88977457c9bd57cb0d.png)

> Over 1 million spine surgeries are performed annually in the U.S., but success rates are low, leading to repeat operations. Proprio’s Paradigm is an FDA-cleared, real-time 3D surgical guidance system to improve outcomes. It uses arrays of sensors and cameras to build a live digital twin of the patient’s body that acts as a map—and also requires fewer traditional scans, thus reducing radiation exposure. The platform has already been used in over 100 procedures with revision-surgery risk dropping from about 50% to less than 10%, the company says. After its first clearance in 2023, Paradigm has rolled out to top medical centers such as Duke Health.

> 美国每年进行超过 100 万例脊柱手术，但成功率低，导致重复手术。Proprio 的 Paradigm 是经 FDA 批准的实时 3D 手术引导系统，可改善手术效果。它使用传感器和摄像头阵列来构建患者身体的实时数字孪生，充当地图——并且还需要更少的传统扫描，从而减少辐射暴露。该公司表示，该平台已在 100 多例手术中使用，修复手术风险从约 50% 降至 10% 以下。在 2023 年首次获得批准后，Paradigm 已推广到杜克健康等顶级医疗中心。
