## U.S. Cyber Trust Mark 美国网络信任标志 - 互联网连接设备的营养标签

> The U.S. Cyber Trust Mark is the government’s bid to bring nutrition-label clarity to connected devices. [Unveiled last year](https://www.cybersecuritydive.com/news/fcc-cyber-trust-mark-ul-investigation-delay/758507/), devices like smart doorbells, routers, and baby monitors can carry a QR-coded shield logo certifying they meet baseline cybersecurity standards set by the FCC and NIST, including consumer-friendly security controls and automatic updates. The label, when scanned, will tell customers security-related information about that specific product including a configuration manual. As of September, the project appeared to be on hold as the FCC had launched an investigation into the private company that vets the project, though experts say delays in implementing the cybersecurity solution puts Americans at risk. The FCC did not respond to requests for comment about the status of the program.

> 美国网络信任标志是美国政府为提高联网设备营养标签清晰度而推出的举措。该标志于去年推出，智能门铃、路由器和婴儿监视器等设备可以带有二维码盾牌标识，证明其符合美国联邦通信委员会 (FCC) 和美国国家标准与技术研究院 (NIST) 制定的网络安全基准标准，包括用户友好的安全控制和自动更新功能。扫描该标签后，消费者将获得该产品的安全相关信息，包括配置手册。截至 9 月，该项目似乎处于搁置状态，因为 FCC 已对负责审查该项目的私营公司展开调查，但专家表示，网络安全解决方案实施的拖延将美国人置于风险之中。FCC 尚未回应关于该项目进展的置评请求。
