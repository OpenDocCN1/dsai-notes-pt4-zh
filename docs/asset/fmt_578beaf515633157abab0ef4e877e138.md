
# 【AI 绘画案例】MJ 中画出梵高风格的图

> 来源：[https://my.feishu.cn/docx/GEundevMDoI6UIxHUghchLFXn3b](https://my.feishu.cn/docx/GEundevMDoI6UIxHUghchLFXn3b)

梵高（Vincent van Gogh）是 19 世纪荷兰著名后印象派画家。梵高以其独特的画风和色彩运用而著称，其作品具有很高的艺术感染力和美感，深受世人喜爱。

除了很有名的星月夜那些作品，梵高也写生过绿意盎然的乡间。

## 一、在 midjourney 里尝试还原一下这种感觉的画

提示词：

```
Capture a night sky swirling with emotional intensity, using hues of blue and yellow, reminiscent of Van Gogh's Starry Night. The landscape below is a quiet, rural town, with lights twinkling in the darkness, a common subject matter in Van Gogh's work. Medium: Digital art. Style: Emulate Van Gogh's unique post-impressionist style, with bold, dramatic, and textured brush strokes that are visible and create a sense of depth. Use asymmetry, diagonal lines, and dynamic shapes to add movement and energy to the artwork. The lines should be organic and flowing, emulating the art nouveau style. Colors: Use of vibrant blues and yellows, with touches of white for the stars, and a combination of warm and cool colors to create depth and intensity, reflecting Van Gogh's use of bold and contrasting colors. --ar 16:9 --v 5.2
```

## 二、重点提示词：

1. 风格：模仿梵高独特的后印象派风格，使用大胆、戏剧性、质感的笔触，这些笔触是可见的，能创造出深度感。

   Style: Emulate Van Gogh's unique post-impressionist style, with bold, dramatic, and textured brush strokes that are visible and create a sense of depth.

2. 使用不对称、对角线和动态形状来增加艺术作品的动感和能量，

   Use asymmetry, diagonal lines, and dynamic shapes to add movement and energy to the artwork.

3. 线条应该是有机和流动的，模仿艺术新青年风格。

   The lines should be organic and flowing, emulating the art nouveau style.

4. 颜色：描述颜色，反映梵高使用大胆和对比色的风格。

   Colors: Use of vibrant blues and yellows, ... reflecting Van Gogh's use of bold and contrasting colors.
