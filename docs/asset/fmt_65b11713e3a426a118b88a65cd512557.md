
如果想知道会发生什么，请运行代码。

Or, read the documentation.

或者，阅读文档。

Or, run the code through a debugger.

或者，通过调试器运行代码。

Or, instrument the code with `print` statements.

或者，使用 `print` 语句检测代码。

If that doesn't work, explain why in your email.

如果这不起作用，请在电子邮件中解释原因。

## Keep it short 简明扼要

An email should be as short as possible.

电子邮件应尽可能简短。

Try living with the five sentences rule for a week.

试着用五句话的规则生活一个星期。

Hone your brevity on twitter.

在推特上磨练你的简洁。

## Subject 主题

A subject should be informative, and about 72 characters or less.

主题应该内容丰富，大约 72 个字符或更少。

If the entire email fits in the subject, put it in the subject.

如果整封电子邮件都适合主题，请将其放在主题中。

If you think that's rude, it's not.

如果你认为这很粗鲁，那就不是了。

If you insist that it's rude, put "Thanks!" in the body.

如果你坚持认为这是不礼貌的，那就把“谢谢！”放在正文里。

If the email fits in the subject, it takes a click out of processing it, and raises the probability of a reply.

如果电子邮件符合主题，则无需点击即可处理它，并提高回复的可能性。

(If the recipient gets hundreds of emails per day, clicks add up.)

（如果收件人每天收到数百封电子邮件，则点击次数会加起来。

If the email doesn't fit in the subject, the subject should contain the most critical details, such as the date, time and location of a meeting, or the top action item and deadline.

如果电子邮件不适合主题，则主题应包含最关键的详细信息，例如会议的日期、时间和地点，或最重要的操作项和截止日期。

For example, don't send "Save the Date" as a subject.

例如，不要将“保存日期”作为主题发送。

Send "Event Title, Save the Date: Date."

发送“事件标题，保存日期：日期”。

In short, the subject must provide enough information for the recipient to know how to prioritize and act on an email quickly.

简而言之，主题必须提供足够的信息，以便收件人知道如何快速确定电子邮件的优先级并采取行动。

## Points, not paragraphs 点，而不是段落

Paragraphs work well for essays.

段落适用于论文。

If your email is an essay, go ahead and use paragraphs.

如果您的电子邮件是一篇文章，请继续使用段落。

If not, put a blank line after every point, which usually means after every independent thought, and certainly after every question.

如果没有，请在每个点之后加一个空行，这通常意味着在每次独立思考之后，当然在每个问题之后。

In short, shape the text to indicate the structure of your message.

简而言之，塑造文本以指示消息的结构。

Strategic whitespace makes a document less threatening to a reader.

战略空白使文档对读者的威胁较小。

Large blocks of text short-circuit reader attention.

大块文本短路读者注意。

## Reply to points 回复积分

If you want to have a discussion, don't put it on top of the message.

如果您想进行讨论，请不要将其放在消息的顶部。

Reply to its points.

回复其观点。

## Place action items on top 将操作项放在顶部

If there are things that the recipient must do, place them up top instead of burying them in the body. Many email clients give a preview of the top part of the message, so these are likely to be seen.

如果有些事情是接受者必须做的，请将它们放在顶部，而不是将它们埋在身体中。许多电子邮件客户端提供邮件顶部的预览，因此可能会看到这些内容。

That is, try sending the following:

也就是说，尝试发送以下内容：

Action item: Send nominees for `X` before date `Y`.

page of text on who is eligible for `X`

not:  不：

page of text on who is eligible for `X`

Can you send nominees for `X` before date `Y`?

## Sorting points by priority 按优先级对点进行排序

Assume that only the top 5 lines of any email will be read.

假设仅阅读任何电子邮件的前 5 行。

Put anything critical in those lines.

把任何关键的东西放在这些行中。

For the remainder, points should be sorted by most to least important.

对于其余部分，应按最重要到最不重要的顺序排序。

## Reply to all or reply to sender? 全部回复还是回复发件人？

When replying to an email, click reply to all.

回复电子邮件时，单击“全部回复”。

After you've written the email, decide who in the recipients list is still a necessary recipient, and delete the rest.

写完电子邮件后，确定收件人列表中的哪些人仍是必要的收件人，并删除其余收件人。

Add anyone that has become relevant.

添加任何相关人员。

## Breaking up emails 分解电子邮件

If you're emailing the same person about several distinct topics, it is usually a good idea to split the email apart into individually processable emails.

如果您要就多个不同的主题向同一个人发送电子邮件，通常最好将电子邮件拆分为可单独处理的电子邮件。

## Manners 礼仪

Don't forget to add "Please" or "Thank you" when and where appropriate.

不要忘记在适当的时间和地点添加“请”或“谢谢”。

A "Thanks!" often comes off as more sincere than "Sincerely."

“谢谢！”往往比“真诚”更真诚。

# 9 How to get a great letter of recommendation

> `https://matt.might.net/articles/how-to-recommendation-letter/`

It's job-hunting and grad-school admissions season.

现在是求职和研究生入学的季节。

As a professor, that means it's time to write recommendation letters.

作为一名教授，这意味着是时候写推荐信了。

Professors enjoy writing letters for excellent students, in part to "pay it back" for all those letters we've had to ask for (and still ask for).

教授们喜欢为优秀的学生写信，部分原因是为了“偿还”我们不得不要求（并且仍然要求）的所有信件。

But, one issue that's become painfully apparent to me is that most students don't know how to get the best possible letter of recommendation.

但是，对我来说，一个显而易见的问题是，大多数学生不知道如何获得最好的推荐信。

If you want good recommendation letters, you will need to plan at least a semester (and preferably a year or two) in advance.

如果你想要好的推荐信，你需要提前至少一个学期（最好是一两年）计划。

If the first time you think about getting a letter of recommendation is when you're applying to a job or grad school, then I must be honest, and I can offer only unpleasant news: you will probably not get a great letter.

如果你第一次考虑收到推荐信是在你申请工作或研究生院的时候，那么我必须说实话，我只能提供一个令人不快的消息：你可能不会收到一封好信。

(But, you can still avoid getting a bad one.)

（但是，你仍然可以避免得到一个坏的。

Getting great letters requires preparation.

获得好信需要准备。

In short, you will have had to engage your letter-writer in some meaningful capacity beyond just taking his or her class and getting an A.

简而言之，你将不得不以某种有意义的身份让你的写信人参与进来，而不仅仅是参加他或她的课程并获得 A。

## Ask early 尽早询问

Do not wait until right before the deadline. Ask at least three weeks in advance, and perhaps six.

不要等到截止日期之前。至少提前三周询问，也许是六周。

## Don't be shy 不要害羞

Do not be shy about asking for letters.

不要羞于索要信件。

Remember that everyone you ask for a letter once had to ask for letters themselves. We remember what it was like.

请记住，您曾经要求写信的每个人都必须自己要求写信。我们记得当时的样子。

I hated asking for letters because it seemed like such an imposition, and I hate to impose. So, I'd often end up waiting to ask.

我讨厌要信，因为这似乎是一种强加，我讨厌强加。所以，我经常会等着问。

Sitting on the other side of the desk, I realize that writing letters is something we sign up for with the job. Asking a professor to write a letter is asking a professor to do his or her job.

坐在桌子的另一边，我意识到写信是我们签下这份工作的对象。请教授写信就是要求教授做他或她的工作。

Personally, I don't mind writing letters, and for good students, I enjoy it.

就我个人而言，我不介意写信，对于好学生，我喜欢它。

## Pick your letter-writers well 好好挑选你的写信人

If you're applying to grad school, you should seek letters from strong names in the field in which you wish to do research.

如果你正在申请研究生院，你应该在你希望做研究的领域寻求知名人士的来信。

You can get a sense of strength from recent publications: Are they publishing frequently and/or recently in top venues for their field?

你可以从最近的出版物中获得一种力量感：他们是否经常和/或最近在他们所在领域的顶级场所发表文章？

If you're applying to a job, the names are almost certainly going to be meaningless to employers, so you'll want letters from the best writers.

如果你正在申请一份工作，这些名字几乎肯定会对雇主毫无意义，所以你会想要最好的作家的来信。

Writing quality and lecturing ability are roughly correlated.

写作质量和讲课能力大致相关。

## Don't get n letters 不要得到 `n` 个字母

Too often, students need `n` letter-writers, so they ask any `n` previous professors for a letter.

很多时候，学生需要 `n` 封信人，所以他们向任何 `n` 名以前的教授索要一封信。

This will get a student an "nth" letter.

这将使学生获得“第 `n` 个”字母。

Professors don't mind writing these letters, because they take about ten seconds. But, deep down, we know we're doing the student a disservice.

教授们不介意写这些信，因为它们大约需要十秒钟。但是，在内心深处，我们知道我们正在伤害学生。

We have templates where we drop in the applicant's name and grade, and coded pleasantries signalling that we have no substantial knowledge of the applicant other than their grade.

我们有模板，其中我们删除了申请人的姓名和等级，以及编码的寒暄，表明除了他们的成绩之外，我们对申请人没有实质性的了解。

These letters won't hurt you, but they won't distinguish you.

这些字母不会伤害你，但它们不会区分你。

Your letters should not rehearse your transcript in long form.

你的信件不应该排练你的长篇大论。

For applications to grad school, if all letters read like this, it guarantees rejection. The message is: "This is a student that did well, but never took the time to go above and beyond."

对于研究生院的申请，如果所有信件都这样读，它肯定会被拒绝。信息是：“这是一个做得很好的学生，但从来没有花时间超越。

## How to get a good letter 如何获得一封好信
