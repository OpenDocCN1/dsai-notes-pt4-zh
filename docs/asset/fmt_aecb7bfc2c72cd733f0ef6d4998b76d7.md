## MDCF-2 - 重要的营养不良补充剂

> Severe childhood malnutrition causes [long-term](https://time.com/7306325/medical-consequences-starvation-gaza/) chronic health conditions, partly because of maldevelopment of the gut microbiome. So Tahmeed Ahmed from Bangladesh’s ICDDR and Jeffrey Gordon of Washington University in St. Louis formulated a therapeutic food—microbiota-directed complementary food No. 2 (MDCF-2)—to encourage production of specific intestinal bacteria that break down food into molecules linked to bone growth and brain development. Clinical trials have shown that malnourished children eating MDCF-2 see bigger weight and height improvements, and an October 2024 study identified a key growth-inducing enzyme the therapy appears to stimulate. The next step, says Gordon, is to get the treatment to “larger populations of children…where acute malnutrition is rampant.”
>
> 严重的儿童营养不良会导致长期慢性健康问题，部分原因是肠道微生物群发育不良。因此，孟加拉国国际营养不良研究中心的 Tahmeed Ahmed 和圣路易斯华盛顿大学的 Jeffrey Gordon 研制出了一种治疗性食品——微生物导向补充食品 2 号 (MDCF-2)——以促进特定肠道细菌的产生，这些细菌将食物分解成与骨骼生长和大脑发育相关的分子。临床试验表明，食用 MDCF-2 的营养不良儿童体重和身高改善更为显著，而 2024 年 10 月的一项研究发现了一种该疗法似乎可以刺激的关键生长诱导酶。Gordon 表示，下一步是将这种治疗方法推广到“更多严重营养不良的儿童群体”。

## Hexavalent vaccines 六价疫苗 - 一次接种预防六种疾病

![](img/c8b944813dfcd6ccdaf8014c404927f3.png)

> [Earlier](https://www.gavi.org/vaccineswork/lifesaving-six-one-vaccine-has-started-roll-out-low-income-countries-heres-why-its) this year, Senegal and Mauritania became the first two low-income countries to distribute lifesaving hexavalent vaccines. The six-in-one vaccine combines two vaccines currently supplied to low-income countries: a pentavalent vaccine that targets diphtheria, pertussis, tetanus, hepatitis B, and meningitis, as well as a separate polio vaccine. Because the hexavalent vaccine simplifies dosing schedules—it involves three injections rather than five spread across two vaccines—more young children will gain protection against polio in particular, according to Gavi, the global health organization that covered the majority of the vaccine’s costs in the two countries. Hexavalent vaccines have been in use in Europe and the U.S. for 25 and 13 years, respectively. It wasn’t fair that “lower-income countries have had to wait,” says Katy Clark, Gavi senior programme manager.
>
> 今年早些时候，塞内加尔和毛里塔尼亚成为首批分发救生六价疫苗的两个低收入国家。这种六合一疫苗结合了目前供应给低收入国家的两种疫苗：一种针对白喉、百日咳、破伤风、乙型肝炎和脑膜炎的五价疫苗，以及一种单独的脊髓灰质炎疫苗。全球卫生组织 Gavi 承担了这两国疫苗的大部分费用。据该组织称，由于六价疫苗简化了接种时间表——只需注射三次，而不是两种疫苗分开接种共五次——特别是更多幼儿将获得针对脊髓灰质炎的保护。六价疫苗在欧洲和美国分别已使用 25 年和 13 年。Gavi 高级项目主管 Katy Clark 表示，低收入国家不得不等待这是不公平的。
