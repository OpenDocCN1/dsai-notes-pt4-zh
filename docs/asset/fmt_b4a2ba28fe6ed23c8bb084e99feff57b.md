# CodeRWA – Pitch Deck (YC Style)

From Code to Capital.

Empowering builders to issue the next generation of digital assets.

---

## Slide 1: What We Do

**CodeRWA**: empowers AI and tech founders to tokenize their code, intellectual property, and future value—transforming developers into capital builders on-chain— enabling global investors to access structured, compliant financial products previously locked in private deals.

---

## Slide 2: The Problem

Tech innovation outpaces capital infrastructure.

+ Early-stage tech teams create high-potential but unpriced digital assets — including code, models, software products, licensing rights, and innovation-driven revenue.
+ No standard structure exists for converting these into investable, compliant financial rights.
+ Digital creators and startup teams lack a platform for verifiable value issuance and structured RWA packaging.
+ Digital innovation assets — including code, algorithms, innovation capabilities, project outcomes, and organizational value structures — remain non-bankable and unliquidated.
+ Investors have limited visibility and infrastructure to access Asia’s emerging digital asset class — especially intangible innovation rights.

---

## Slide 3: Our Solution

+ Build structured RWA rights (dividends, licensing, future cash flows) around digital innovation assets — including code, innovation capabilities, project outcomes, and organizational value structures.
+ Create a new class of digital-native capital markets centered on technical creativity.
+ STO issuance platform to structure dividends, voting, and yield-sharing
+ Project screening and verification system to ensure credible, developer-backed assets
+ **Investor Portal** for LPs, FO, ventures and syndicates to access structured exposure

---

## Slide 4: Why Now

+ $95B+ in global AI funding (2024), yet Asia-based teams remain capital-starved
+ Thousands of unmonetized AI/code/IP projects remain off the financial grid
+ Tech IP like code & models lack structured liquidity — CodeRWA provides that layer
+ HK, Singapore, UAE are clarifying STO frameworks, enabling compliant offerings
+ Post-ZIRP investors seek programmable yield — revenue-tokenized tech is next

---

## Slide 5: Market

RWA Market (Top-down):

+ $16T+ by 2030 (BCG)
+ $3.5T tokenized by 2030 (base case)

Tech IP Liquidity (Bottom-up):

+ 30M+ global devs, $1.2T IP value/year
+ Tens of thousands of projects eligible for token-based financing
+ Rising demand from LPs for equity-adjacent income instruments

---

## Slide 6: Product

CodeRWA Infrastructure

+ Digital Asset Registry for digital innovation assets — including code, algorithms, models, innovation capabilities, project outcomes, and organizational value structures — with provenance, contribution lineage, and version tracking
+ STO Packaging Engine for structuring equity, revenue-sharing, licensing, and dividend rights across various digital innovation asset classes
+ **Smart Contracts** with composable modules for vesting, revenue flows, transfer restrictions, and role-based permissions
+ Investor Dashboard with real-time asset performance tracking, payout status, governance interaction, and redemption triggers
+ **Issuer Portal** for onboarding, document submission, and token structure configuration; supports integration with custodians and law firms
+ Valuation Engine combining asset history, usage metrics, and community contribution graphs to generate transparent, on-chain price signals
+ Security & Compliance Layer including programmable access control, jurisdictional rulesets, and audit-ready issuance logs

> Visual: Platform flow chart showing AI/Tech Company → CodeRWA → Stock / Dividends / Future Income → STO Token → Global Investor
