
The more I know about an applicant, the better the letter.

我对申请人了解得越多，这封信就越好。

When you take a class from someone that you want to be a letter-writer, distinguish yourself with your level of effort, your helpfulness to other students, your interaction inside and outside the classroom, and your enthusiasm for the subject.

当你从你想成为写信人的人那里上课时，要用你的努力程度、你对其他学生的帮助、你在课堂内外的互动以及你对这个主题的热情来区分自己。

At a minimum, provide a recommender with some context: a capsule summary of your interests/accomplishments, your resume/CV, any application materials you are submitting and a list of places to which you plan to apply.

至少，向推荐人提供一些背景信息：您的兴趣/成就的胶囊摘要、您的简历/简历、您提交的任何申请材料以及您计划申请的地方列表。

## How to get a great letter 如何获得一封好信

If you want a great letter, do independent study or research with the letter-writer for a semester or two before asking for the letter.

如果你想要一封好信，在索要这封信之前，请与写信人一起独立学习或研究一两个学期。

Those one-on-ones will give the letter-writer valuable insight into your character, and if the projects succeed (yielding a publication), then your letter-writer will be able to write an enthusiastic endorsement.

这些一对一的谈话将使写信人对你的性格有宝贵的了解，如果项目成功（产生出版物），那么你的写信人将能够写出热情的背书。

Research is always risky, so if you want a publication with your letter-writer, start working with them at least a year (and probably two) in advance.

研究总是有风险的，所以如果你想和你的写信人一起发表文章，至少提前一年（可能是两年）开始与他们合作。

Beyond netting a great letter, doing one-on-one work grants indirect access to the professor's network of personal contacts at other universities.

除了写一封好信之外，一对一的工作还可以间接访问教授在其他大学的个人联系网络。

## How to ask for a letter 如何索要一封信

There is a debate about whether you should ask for a letter in person or over email. Personally, I don't think it matters that much.

关于您是应该亲自还是通过电子邮件索要一封信，存在争议。就个人而言，我认为这并不重要。

Whether over email or in person, what you should ask is, "Would you be comfortable writing me a strong letter of recommendation?"

无论是通过电子邮件还是当面，你应该问的是，“你愿意给我写一封强有力的推荐信吗？

Almost no professor will turn down a request for a letter, but if you ask for a strong letter, you might hear back, "I don't think I can write the strongest letter--who else are you asking to write letters?"

几乎没有教授会拒绝写信的请求，但如果你要求一封强有力的信，你可能会听到回复，“我认为我不能写出最强烈的信——你还要求谁写信？

When I turn down such requests, I then help the student find who their strongest letter writers would be.

当我拒绝这样的请求时，我会帮助学生找到他们最强大的写信人。

Over email, include a brief capsule summary of interests and accomplishements, as well as a link to your resume/CV and application materials.

通过电子邮件，包括兴趣和成就的简短摘要，以及指向您的简历/简历和申请材料的链接。

If a letter must be mailed, include a pre-addressed, pre-stamped envelope and any other materials required.

如果必须邮寄信件，请附上预先写好地址、预先盖章的信封和任何其他所需的材料。

## How to follow up 如何跟进

Professors generally won't forget to write a letter, but we are never annoyed by a brief reminder (or two) from the applicant.

教授通常不会忘记写信，但我们永远不会因为申请人的简短提醒而烦恼。

Do send reminder emails a week before each deadline.

请在每个截止日期前一周发送提醒电子邮件。

Lately, I've asked applicants to create a shared google spreadsheet with organizations and deadlines that I can update when I submit letters. Feel free to offer this up front.

最近，我要求申请人创建一个与组织和截止日期共享的谷歌电子表格，我可以在提交信件时更新该电子表格。请随时提前提供。

This will give you peace of mind, in that you can verify whether or not a professor has submitted a letter. It also gives you a polite way to ask a follow-up: "I see that you haven't checked off the spreadsheet for X and it's due in three days. Did you forget to check that off?"

这将使您高枕无忧，因为您可以验证教授是否提交了一封信。它还为您提供了一种礼貌的方式来询问后续问题：“我看到您还没有在电子表格中检查 X，并且三天后到期。你忘了检查吗？

## It's been a while, can I still ask? 已经有一段时间了，我还能问吗？

[Added](`http://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=3163691`) in response to questions on HN.

在回答有关HN的问题时添加。

If you've graduated and gone to industry, you can still ask for a letter.

如果你已经毕业并进入了工业界，你仍然可以要求一封信。

When you email the professor, include a short, bulleted update with what you've done since graduating.

当你给教授发电子邮件时，包括一个简短的、有项目符号的更新，说明你自毕业以来所做的事情。

Emphasize how the specific techniques learned in their class or in independent study have been invaluable in your career. Include examples.

强调在他们的课堂或独立学习中学到的具体技术在您的职业生涯中是无价的。包括示例。

If you don't hear back, don't give up. Email again after a week.

如果您没有收到回复，请不要放弃。一周后再次发送电子邮件。

Most professors have hopelessly swollen inboxes, and expect to be re-emailed with anything critical.

大多数教授的收件箱都无可救药地膨胀了，并希望收到任何关键内容的重新电子邮件。

## Should I draft my own letter? 我应该起草自己的信吗？

[Added](`http://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=3163691`) in response to questions on HN.

在回答有关HN的问题时添加。

If the professor requests a draft, yes.

如果教授要求草稿，是的。

Do not offer to write the letter up front, but if you know the professor is particularly busy, you can append "Please let me know if there's anything else I can do to help you draft the letter" to your request.

不要主动提出提前写这封信，但如果你知道教授特别忙，你可以在你的要求中附上“如果我还能做些什么来帮助你起草这封信，请告诉我”。

Typically, what the professor is looking for is a list of bullet points about interests and accomplishments that they can massage into a letter.

通常，教授正在寻找的是关于兴趣和成就的要点列表，他们可以将其整理成一封信。

To expedite the letter, be sure to ask in what format they want the draft, e.g., Word, LaTeX or plaintext.

为了加快这封信的处理速度，一定要询问他们想要什么格式的草稿，例如 Word、LaTeX 或纯文本。

## Tip: Send a thank you note 提示：发送感谢信

It's always good to follow-up with a thank you note so they know where you were accepted, and where not. This makes it easier to ask if you need another recommendation in the future too!

跟进一封感谢信总是好的，这样他们就知道你在哪里被录取了，在哪里没有被录取。这样可以更轻松地询问您将来是否需要其他推荐！
