## In-Editor  编辑内

Chat can also be used as an editor tab by clicking on the horizontal “More” button, and then clicking on the “Open Chat in Editor” button. Chat will then behave as a regular editor tab. Pressing Ctrl/⌘ + L will bring up the chat tab and focus onto it.

聊天也可以通过点击水平方向的“更多”按钮，然后点击“在编辑器中打开聊天”按钮，用作编辑器标签页。此时，聊天将表现得像一个常规的编辑器标签页。按下 Ctrl/⌘ + L 将弹出聊天标签页并聚焦到它上。
