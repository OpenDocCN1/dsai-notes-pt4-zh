# 7 Powerful GPT‑5 Prompts for Instant Upgrade

> 来源：[https://my.feishu.cn/docx/LsGTdfFO5oGk0TxiCMScBhxnnpQ](https://my.feishu.cn/docx/LsGTdfFO5oGk0TxiCMScBhxnnpQ)

**使用场景或用例：**适用于希望 GPT‑5 在日常生活、工作计划、组织、家庭活动推荐等多个场景中提升效率和实用性的用户，通过结构化 prompt 优化生产力体验。

**提示词：**

```
Scheduling and workflow – Create realistic, step‑by‑step weekly schedules balancing work, parenting, and fitness.
Organization – Categorize and clean up cluttered Google Drive folders based on naming patterns and redundancy.
Family life – Plan full‑day family activities tailored to specific age groups and budgets.
Lifestyle &amp; health – Transform grocery lists into balanced weekly meal plans optimized for freshness.
Culture &amp; entertainment – Recommend books based on a user’s favorite TV shows using cultural mapping.
Social skills – Generate context‑specific conversation starters to help in new social settings.
Pop culture updates – Translate trending topics into easy‑to‑digest summaries for users who are busy or offline.
```
