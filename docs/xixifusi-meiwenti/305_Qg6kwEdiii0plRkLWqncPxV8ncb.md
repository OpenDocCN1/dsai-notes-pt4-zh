# 为什么 Startup 永远有机会

1.  Startups disrupt established players with their agility, innovation, and ability to adapt to changing market dynamics.

1.  Entrepreneurs should identify and exploit the innovator's dilemma in their industries, finding opportunities where big companies lack the necessary data or vision to compete with startups.
