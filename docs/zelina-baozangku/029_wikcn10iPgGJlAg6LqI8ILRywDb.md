## 一、Part1 Interview

### 1. 考试内容

在 `part1` 中，主要有两个阶段。第一个阶段是大概 30s 的自我介绍，第二个阶段是每个人回答考官的 4 个问题。顺序一般为：

自我介绍A → 自我介绍B → A回答环节 → B回答环节

### 2. 问答环节

下面给出几个问答环节的练习题目。具体看下方👇

#### （1）问题1

Question:

+   How would you feel about having a job interview by phone?

你认为电话面试是怎么样的呢？

Answer:

+   I believe having a job interview by phone has positive effects on both the interviewer and the interviewee.

+   For the interviewer, a job interview by phone is quicker and more convenient than arranging a face-to-face meeting, especially for a preliminary stage of recruitment.

+   For the interviewee, a job interview on the phone is fair because the usual visual impression is absent.

+   Interviewee can focus on showing the ability and commitment during the conversation.

#### （2）问题2

Question:

+   What advice do you think would be useful for someone who is going to be interviewed by phone?

Answer:

+   Being interviewed by phone is quite different from attending a face-to-face interview.

+   The first to bear in mind is to be well prepared.

+   The telephone interview would not leave much time for the interviewee.

+   Getting ready by things listing key criteria of the job before the interview will be critical.

+   Another thing to remember is to keep calm during the interview.

+   Unexpected things may occur during the interview.

+   The interviewer may not be heard clearly or the communication failure may occur.

+   Keeping calm is therefore suggested for all situations.

#### （3）问题3

Question:

+   Why do you think some companies prefer to do job interviews by phone?

Answer:

+   I believe some companies decide to do job interviews by phone in order to save time and money.

+   In the preliminary stage of recruitment, a telephone conversation is an effective way to access the general ability and social skill of interviewees in a shorter period.

+   Other companies choose to do job interviews by phone to find serious applicants.

+   Only applicants that are willing to be interviewed by phone and also getting ready to it should be taken to the process of the job application.

大家可以看到，每一个话题底下都有给出1-2个论点，那是不是在考试当中，一定要围绕这两个论点来作答呢？答案肯定是否定的。

在实际的考试当中，同学们可以依据自己的需求来对论点进行挑选，新增或者删除等操作。

通常来讲，题目给出的论点会比较好展开讲解，所以能用题目的尽量用题目的，不要给自己挖坑。

最终的评分只跟自己论述的点是否合理有关系，跟论点是否另辟蹊径没有关系。

### 3. 关于 1P 的准备

在第 1 点中，我们谈论到了 `1P` 是做 1min 的准备和做 1min 的陈述。那接下来，我们来看下，这 1min 的陈述，都应该怎么准备，又应该说些什么呢？

首先，在 1min 的准备时间里，你需要 10s 的时间来决定要讲什么，之后剩余的 50s 就是用来打草稿。在这一部分，需要锻炼的能力是： Training yourself note-writing skills by combing and streamlining the main ideas 。

其次是 1min 的陈述时间。 1min 的陈述时间里，回答结构如下：

```
Opening Sentence(Thesis)
Topic Sentence 1
● Supporting detail-1
● Supporting detail-2
Topic Sentence 2
● Supporting detail-1
● Supporting detail-2
Conclusion
```

在上面这段结构中，大家可以看到，首先我们需要有一个总结性的句子，也就是 `Thesis`。其次呢，对两个论点进行展开细述。

当然，你也可以只有一个论点，进行 detail-3 和 detail-4 的扩充。也可以有 3-4 个论点去进行陈述，这些都依据自己而定。

但在多数情况下，按照上面两个论点的结构是较为合理的。

### 4. 1P如何练习

那对于上面的 `1P` 来说，我们在平时的练习当中，又该如何练习和做笔记呢？

笔记三部曲：

+   决定目标（What you can say）

+   列出主要理由 （Topic Sentence Key words）

+   确定 supporting details 拓展内容（清晰的结构）

如果你能说出第一部分和第二部分，那么实际大概就会得到 3 分。如果你把第三部分也陈述出来了，就会获得 4-5 分这样的一个情况。

### 7. 问题汇总 - 1Q

#### （1）万金油答案

下面给出 `1Q` 的万金油问题汇总。具体如下：

基础问题(easy)：

```
Question1:
You have mentioned A,B and C in the presentation. Which one is more important?/Which one you value most?

Question2:
Do you think A is the most important one?
```

进阶问题(Advanced)：

```
Question1:
You mentioned A,B and C.Do you think(sth related to A,B and C)?
e.g.
You mentioned buiness trip and maitaining customer relationship.Do you think you should be trained about the other cultures where you&#x27;re going to do business trips?
```

#### （2）注意点

对于中等水平的问答来说，主要要抓住细节和抓住主题。具体如下：

+   抓住细节： what 、 how 、 why 、 when 、 where 。

+   抓到主题： Do you believe/think/agree that…? 、 Will you be willing to …?

---

对于进阶水平的问答来说，主要包括正反分析、昨天今天明天、问题解决和因果关系。比如：

+   正反分析 - What's the advantage of …?

+   昨天今天明天 - What are the differences between A now and that of future?

+   问题解决 - What might be the biggest trouble for …? What's your possible solution?

+   因果关系 - What are the causes of …?

### 10. 实战演练

下面，我们用几道题来演练一下模拟一下实际的回答。

#### （1）题目一

先给出题目：

```
Planning a working day
- Making a list tasks
- Deciding when to answer emails
```

附上答案：

```
When we are ready to plan a working day, there are serveral points that we should keep in mind to make sure everything goes well.

①The first thing to remember is to make a list of tasks.
Making a to-do list can help people organize things at work. This is especially important when we plan a working day following the holiday, when important tasks can be easily forgotten.
②Emails can be also very crtical when we are doing business.
We should spare a certain period of time at work to deal with emails from our colleagues, supervisors and also from business partners.
③The last thing to bear in mind is to prioritize things at work. 
Urgent things should always come first. To prioritize tasks well is the key to efficiency. 

These points should be carefully considered when we make working day plans in order to have a productive day at work.
```

#### （2）题目二

先给出题目：



```
Choosing an agency to recruit staff
- Reputation of agency
- The agency's charges
```

**附上答案：**

```
When I choose an agency to recruit staff, some things should be of great importance.

①The first thing is the reputation of the agency.
Recruiting staff can be a complex business so finding an agency with a good reputation in this business is critical.
This can be done through gathering information from other companies or looking for help from an independent consultant.
②Another thing to bear in mind is the charges of the agency.
In many cases, the human resource department is seen as a cost center in the company and the budget will be controlled.
Thus, to consider the cost of the agency within the certain budget is highly appreciated by the company.

Finding the balance of the reputation and the charges of the agency can be hard, but it is indeed important.
```

### （3）题目三

**先给出题目：**

```
Replacing a computer system
- Potential benefits
- Quality of technical consultants
```

**附上答案：**

```
When I am ready to replace a computer system, I believe it is important to consider the following issues.

①The first one is the potential benefits. Replacing a computer system means a lot of workload. Thus the new computer system must bring some benefits, such as better technical performance and lower costs.
②Another thing worth serious consideration is the quality of technical consultants.
Computer systems require well-equipped technical consultants. This is especially import for small companies who do not have much access to technical support. In this case, technical consultancy can be of high significance.

Replacing a computer system is no easy task and these things are what I need to consider.
```

# 三、Part3 Collaborative task and Follow-up Question

## 1. 题目模拟

我们先用一套题目来演示 part 3 怎么考。**具体如下：**

+   主题 Sales Catalogue: Marketing

+   Your department is producing a sales catalogue to promote a new range of high quality watches and jewellery. （情景）

+   You have been asked for your suggestions about the catalogue.（情景参与者）

+   Discuss the sutuaion together and decide:（任务）

+   what information on include in the catalogue（要解决的问题）

+   how to make the catalogue attractive to customers（要解决的问题）

不同于 part2 的是， part3 当中有两个**要解决的问题**，那在实际考试中，这两个问题是**必须都要回答的**。而在 part2 中，提供的点子仅是参考的点子，可回答也可不回答。

## 2. Tips

下面给出一些关于 part3 中对话的一些小技巧 Tips 。**具体如下：**

+   快速了解任务要求

+   组织良好且逻辑有序 well-organized && logically ordered

+   快速进入角色/场景 our company / our service / our staff

+   学会问问题

+   学会礼貌而不失尴尬的不同意

+   合理分配时间，解决提示卡上的问题

+   如果 3min 内讨论完了，可以适当总结一下重点

## 3. 如何打开话题

**对于这**部分来说，打开话题可以有以下几种方式。比如：

+   To begin with, how would you like …?

+   First, what do you think would be …?

+   First, let's have a look at …

要以一种**邀请的态度**或者是**我想听听你怎么说**的方式来打开话题。
