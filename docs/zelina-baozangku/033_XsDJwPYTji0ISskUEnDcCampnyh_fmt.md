# 1 Company Operation and Management

## 1.1 Teamwork

### （1）Collaborative Task

**Task👇🏻：**

```
A Good Team Leader
The company you work for is thinking of selecting a new team leader. You have been asked to make suggestions about the making of a good team leader.
Discuss the situation together, and decide:
▷ what personal qualities a team leader should possess
▷ What leadership style a good team leader should choose?
```

Suggested Answer👇🏻：

```
A:** I think the **most important personal quality we expect in a good team leader is integrity —— A good leader should be an honest and trustworthy person, he or she should be committed to ethical values.

B: Yes, I agree with you here. Integrity helps to reinforce ethical behaviors among team members and enhances cooperation and builds up a good working atmosphere.

A: Competence is another important leadership quality. A good team leader should be experienced and capable of coordinating tasks the team is supposed to achieve. A competent team leader can always command the attention and respect of the team members so that he or she can direct the team efficiently and provide assistance when necessary.

B: I agree. Decisiveness is also important. A good team leader should have the ability to make decisions quickly based on the information at hand and stick to these decisions. A team leader must constantly tell team members what to do and make decisions about projects. Inability to make the right decisions can hamper project progress. And if the leader goes back on decisions, he can lose his integrity and credibility.

A: You are quite right. A team leader should also be open-minded and easy-going. If workers are afraid to approach the team leader, it can result in communication failure.

B: Yes. Besides all this, creativity and innovative spirit are also expected in a good team leader.

A: Yes, of course. A leader leads based on his strengths, not on his titles. And I should say the leadership style a team leader chooses will have direct impact on the workplace.

B: I think a democratic leadership style is very effective in mobilizing the team toward a common vision. The teammates will be happy to contribute fresh ideas and work together for their shared objectives.

A: Being a good team leader means that he can never give up on the team. A democratic leadership style enables the leader to communicate more effectively with the team and increase work efficiency.

B: Good leaders are always willing to listen. An overbearing leader can only lead to negative team emotions.

A: Yes. A good leader doesn&#x27;t act like a bossy person who&#x27;s in charge of eeryone. He himself is a good team player.
```

> New Word Analysis：

> credibility 可信度

> team player 团队精神

> bossy person 专横的人

> in charge of 掌管

# 2 Human Resource Management

## 2.1 Recruiting & Selecting

### （1）Collaborative Task

**Task👇🏻：**

```
Advertising for job opening
Your company is going to fill the job vacancy of tens of salespersons by advertising. You have been asked to make recommendations. Discuss the situation together and decide:
▷ what type of publication is to choose
▷ what are to be included in the advertisement
```

Suggested Answer👇🏻：

```
A: You see, we’ve decided to open a new store and you are in desperate need of 10 more salesman.

B: It’s a crucial time to consider advertising the vacancy.

A: Then, what kind of publication do you think, should we turn to?

A: When choosing a publication, we should consider its readers. We should make sure whether it is directed to a wide range of readers or just to a particular group. Besides, it’s also important to consider advertising fee. The advertising cost should be reasonable and affordable, somewhere around the company’s budget.

A: Exactly. So, in our case, we should choose a local publication instead of a national one. What do you think of the Gazette?

B: It’s undoubtedly an ideal choice, since it’s the most influential newspaper in our city. Besides, newspaper advertising is the cheapest way of reaching a large number of readers of various backgrounds.

A: Exactly. Furthermore, as an influential newspaper, its advertising staff’s ability and creativity can be guaranteed. Then the money we invest will surely pay off.

B: Yes, it’s important to insure that our advertisement effort is cost-effective. To recruit the right person, we should also consider the contents of the advertisement.

A: In my opinion, besides, the basic information as time, place, and contact address, the description of applicants’ qualification, and the specifications of the openings should be included, because it can cut down the number of applicants and attract the desirable ones.

B: Yeah, to attract ideal applicants, it’s also necessary for our job advertisement to show the specific benefits that our company can offer.

A: Exactly. Besides, this is also a chance to publicize the image of our company. To do this well, the advertisement should be well-designed and cover general information of our company.

B: Good idea.
```

### （2）Dicussion

Follow-up questions👇🏻：

```
▷ Do you think it’s important to consider potentiality of the company when deciding to accept a job offer?

▷ Would you choose TV advertisement for job vacancy?

▷ Is company culture important to you when deciding to accept a job offer?

▷ Would you consider salary when choosing a new job?

▷ What kind of qualities are important to be a salesperson?
```

Suggested Answers👇🏻：

```
（1）Yes. In a company with potentiality, you can expect new opportunities for career advancement, handsome salary and other material success which ensure comfortable life.

（2）Normally not, since TV advertisement can be very expensive. When advertising job opening, we should take budget and the type of job into consideration.

（3）Yes. It is important to consider the company culture, since company culture will determine your job satisfaction and potential development in the company.

（4）Yes. Salary is important for bread and milk and should be reasonable and match what I&#x27;ve&#x27; contributed to the organization.

（5）For a salesperson, being extrovert and good at communicating are crucial, since a salesman needs to influence and pursuade the potential customers.
```
